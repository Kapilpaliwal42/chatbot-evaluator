Quote matching is only an integrity check

Quote integrity counts findings whose quoted text exists as an exact substring in the cited reference. It does not establish that the quote logically supports the finding. Passing this check cannot certify sound reasoning.

A separate agent reviewed 22 calibration disagreement cases. This audit covers a subset of rationales and findings; it does not assess all cases or provide human-approved labels. Suggested verdicts are qualitative review suggestions and do not change the frozen benchmark metrics. A review escalation may remain procedurally justified by invalid or missing evidence even when the finding’s reasoning is faulty.

What these results support

Fine-tuning deferred; need human-approved representative labels and fresh validation.

The calibration-selected judge matched 12 of the 25 heldout labels. It produced 0 false passes among 16 expected fail/review cases, 6 false alarms among 9 expected pass cases, and 0 errors. These are observed sample counts.

  • Have a human approve representative labels and verify findings against their source passages.
  • Diagnose repeated reasoning errors and repair citation/evidence scaffolding after this frozen benchmark, using a new dataset version to evaluate changes.
  • Use a fresh untouched validation set after any prompt, model, or training changes. Keep this frozen holdout separate from training.

No fine-tuning has occurred in this benchmark. Neither software checks nor these synthetic label matches establish readiness for unattended client audits.

Reproducibility details

All four complete runs use the same frozen prompt, evaluator source, Ollama version, and generation settings. Model digests are stable across splits. Policy families are disjoint between calibration and holdout.
